# Jun Miguel Corbilla

### Third-Year Computer Science Student · Exploring Game Development & Cybersecurity

I'm a third-year Computer Science student learning my way through two fields that genuinely interest me: **game development** and **cybersecurity**. I'm still exploring, building a broad foundation, and paying attention to the work that best matches my strengths and the kind of problems I want to spend time solving. I hope to grow steadily, then focus more deeply as my interests and experience become clearer.

---

## 🎯 About Me

Game development draws me in because it brings together code, systems, and experiences people can interact with. Cybersecurity interests me for a different reason: understanding how systems behave, spotting risks, and helping protect the people who rely on them. I'm learning about both before deciding where to specialize, and I want that decision to come from curiosity, practice, and an honest sense of where I can do my best work.

On the cybersecurity side, I'm especially curious about **blue-team work**: monitoring, investigation, and defending systems. In the future, I'd be glad to explore opportunities with Philippine public-sector organizations such as **DICT** or **CICC**. For now, I'm focused on learning the fundamentals and finding out which path fits me best.
